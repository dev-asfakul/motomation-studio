# Style: Aurora

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `aurora.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** the night sky as the clock: curtains of light that follow time.
**Use when:** calm products, sleep, astronomy, night events, slow storytelling.
**Avoid when:** energetic campaigns; dense information.

- **Type:** a light italic serif display, a quiet grotesk.
- **Colour:** deep green-black, aurora green, a violet top, chalk.
- **Layout:** a ledger over a sky: entries in solid panels.
- **Motion:** Tier 3–4. Slow sine eases; one scroll-driven hour.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 3
- **Archetypes that suit it:** ledger, timeline, stage
- **Section order:** dusk · the hours · the peak · dawn
- **Signature menu:** Seismograph Line, Darkroom Develop, Meteors once
- **Must-haves:** text always on solid panels; a CSS poster for the sky
- **Pitfall:** the stock 'aurora gradient' hero behind a SaaS headline.
