# Style: Cyberpunk

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `cyberpunk.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** night, rain, neon and a working terminal.
**Use when:** developer tools, security, games, night events, anything that has a CLI.
**Avoid when:** wellness, children, anything that should feel safe and slow.

- **Type:** a condensed display face in caps, a monospace for everything else.
- **Colour:** near-black ground, one hot neon and one cool counter-neon; no rainbow.
- **Layout:** a console: HUD overlays, a terminal, scanned panels.
- **Motion:** Tier 2–3. Jitter on a fixed clock then still; glitch only on events.
- **Default dials:** Energy 3 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 2
- **Archetypes that suit it:** console, field-guide, ledger
- **Section order:** boot · stakeout · the terminal · the leads
- **Signature menu:** Decode, Scramble on Hover, Terminal
- **Must-haves:** a terminal that actually answers; neon text that passes contrast
- **Pitfall:** permanent glitching and unreadable neon on black.
