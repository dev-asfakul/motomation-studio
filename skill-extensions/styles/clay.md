# Style: Clay

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `clay.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** soft, matte, handmade volume: things you want to squeeze.
**Use when:** kids, food, toys, friendly products, onboarding.
**Avoid when:** serious B2B, legal, medical.

- **Type:** a soft heavy display face, a rounded humanist text face.
- **Colour:** muted earthy colours, low saturation, matte (no gloss).
- **Layout:** a shelf of objects; generous radius; objects sit on a ground line.
- **Motion:** Tier 2. Squash and stretch with volume kept, slow sag back.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 2
- **Archetypes that suit it:** shelf, workbench, room
- **Section order:** the lineup · one object up close · how it's made · the shelf
- **Signature menu:** Hold to Commit, Sticker Scatter, Proximity Weight
- **Must-haves:** area-preserving squash; a texture that reads as matte at every size
- **Pitfall:** gloss and gradients that turn clay into plastic.
