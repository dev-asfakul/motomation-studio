# Style: Retro

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `retro.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** analogue media made interactive: tape, film, grain.
**Use when:** archives, documentaries, music, heritage brands, anniversaries.
**Avoid when:** products that must feel current; dense UI.

- **Type:** a heavy italic display face, a mono counter.
- **Colour:** warm darks and a safelight red; grain at 6–12%.
- **Layout:** a reel or deck: chapters in a 4:3 gate on a wide wall.
- **Motion:** Tier 4–5. Scrubbed timelines, tape inertia, glitch only on fast-forward.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 3 · Motion 3
- **Archetypes that suit it:** film-reel, timeline, poster
- **Section order:** leader · chapter 1 · chapter 2 · chapter 3 · end card
- **Signature menu:** Timecode Rail, Horizontal Reel, Light Sweep
- **Must-haves:** a real timeline you can scrub; a reduced-motion storyboard of still frames
- **Pitfall:** sepia filters and fake VHS noise on everything.
