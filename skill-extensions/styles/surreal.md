# Style: Surreal

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `surreal.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** the familiar made wrong on purpose, so the visitor looks twice.
**Use when:** dream, art, perfume, fiction, a launch that should feel uncanny.
**Avoid when:** trust-critical products (finance, health) or anywhere the visitor must act quickly.

- **Type:** an expressive serif for display, a calm text face; the type stays readable while the world bends.
- **Colour:** one strange light (a cast colour) over natural tones.
- **Layout:** a stage: one impossible scene per screen, with the content held in a stable frame.
- **Motion:** Tier 3–4. Slow melts, gravity flips, impossible transitions, always with a still poster.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 3
- **Archetypes that suit it:** stage, room, specimen
- **Section order:** the impossible scene · the explanation · the evidence · the exit
- **Signature menu:** Aperture, Darkroom Develop, Lens
- **Must-haves:** a plain-language line next to every strange image; a reduced-motion poster that keeps the strangeness
- **Pitfall:** weird for its own sake. The wrongness must mean something in the concept.
