# Style: Vector art

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `vector-art.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** flat shapes, one stroke weight, geometric construction.
**Use when:** explainers, product illustration, identity work, education.
**Avoid when:** photographic or emotive storytelling.

- **Type:** a variable grotesk with a wide width axis.
- **Colour:** 2–3 flat colours plus ink; no gradients.
- **Layout:** a diptych: construction on one side, result on the other.
- **Motion:** Tier 2–3. Clean morphs and stroke draw-on, eased in and out.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 2
- **Archetypes that suit it:** diptych, specimen, catalogue
- **Section order:** the brief · the construction · the swap · the final mark
- **Signature menu:** Letter Relay, Scroll Float, Line Curtain
- **Must-haves:** consistent stroke and corner radius everywhere
- **Pitfall:** generic flat people and blob characters.
