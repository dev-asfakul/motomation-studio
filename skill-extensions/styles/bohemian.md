# Style: Bohemian

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `bohemian.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** warm, layered, botanical and slow.
**Use when:** craft, food, wellness, hospitality, music festivals.
**Avoid when:** finance, enterprise, anything that must feel exact.

- **Type:** a soft, wonky serif display; a warm text serif.
- **Colour:** terracotta, ochre, plum, moss on linen.
- **Layout:** an index of cards; arches; botanical frames.
- **Motion:** Tier 2. Slow sway, gentle lean, soft step back.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 2
- **Archetypes that suit it:** index, shelf, letter
- **Section order:** the reading · the cards · the specimens · the colophon
- **Signature menu:** Considerate Magnet, Curve Carousel, Rotating Words
- **Must-haves:** every decorative plant framing, never under text
- **Pitfall:** pattern overload and unreadable script on paisley.
