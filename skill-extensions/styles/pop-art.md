# Style: Pop art

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `pop-art.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** Ben-Day dots, thick outlines and speech: the page as a comic.
**Use when:** consumer brands, events, campaigns, playful editorial.
**Avoid when:** subdued luxury, sensitive subjects.

- **Type:** a heavy mono or slab display in caps, a sturdy grotesk for bubbles.
- **Colour:** primaries on off-white; dots carry tone.
- **Layout:** comic panels on a 4-up page, numbered.
- **Motion:** Tier 2. Impact then still; one shake on landing.
- **Default dials:** Energy 3 · Rhythm 3 · Motion 2
- **Archetypes that suit it:** lookbook, poster, gallery-wall
- **Section order:** cover · four panels · the punchline · back cover
- **Signature menu:** Proximity Weight on dots, Typewriter, Bento Reveal
- **Must-haves:** halftone from a real source; each panel says one thing
- **Pitfall:** clip-art pastiche and comic fonts for body text.
