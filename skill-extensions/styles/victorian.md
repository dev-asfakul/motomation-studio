# Style: Victorian

> **Standards vs style:** this style may override house-style defaults (canvas, type scale, grid, accent); it never overrides the universal standards in `principles/restraint.md`.
> **Extension:** written for motomation-studio because the skill (v2.6.0) has no `victorian.md`. Same format as `styles/minimal.md`, plus the fields the build brief asked for. Candidate to contribute back.

**Essence:** ornament, engraving and ceremony, under gaslight.
**Use when:** heritage, publishing, spirits, theatre, museums.
**Avoid when:** modern products that need speed and clarity.

- **Type:** a high-contrast Didone display, an old-style text serif.
- **Colour:** near-black, brass, cream paper, one oxblood.
- **Layout:** a poster or archive: framed plates, centred titles.
- **Motion:** Tier 2. Weighted, ceremonial movement, then still.
- **Default dials:** Energy 2 · Rhythm 2 · Motion 2
- **Archetypes that suit it:** poster, catalogue, specimen
- **Section order:** the curtain · the plates · the seals · the colophon
- **Signature menu:** Darkroom Develop, Curved Text, Shiny Text
- **Must-haves:** engraving made from line, not raster texture; ornament at the edges only
- **Pitfall:** ornament on everything; low-contrast brass on black text.
